
# 📊 Market Research Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary

This analysis reviews a sample of 10 biryani listings in Coimbatore to understand differences in pricing, customer ratings, and estimated delivery time.

The objective is to identify pricing patterns and potential value-for-money opportunities.

---

## 2. Price Analysis

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average Listed Price | ₹258.66 |
| Lowest Listed Price | ₹130 |
| Highest Listed Price | ₹350 |
| Price Range | ₹220 |

The selected sample shows a wide pricing range from ₹130 to ₹350.

This indicates that customers in the market have options across budget, mid-range, and premium price segments.

---

## 3. Rating Analysis

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average Rating | 4.3 / 5 |
| Highest Rating | 4.7 / 5 |
| Lowest Rating | 3.9 / 5 |

The average rating of the selected sample is 4.3/5.

Higher pricing does not automatically correspond to higher customer ratings. Some mid-priced restaurants also show strong ratings.

---

## 4. Delivery Time Analysis

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Average Delivery Time | 28.6 minutes |
| Fastest Estimate | 9 minutes |
| Slowest Estimate | 55 minutes |

Delivery speed varies significantly between restaurants.

This suggests that delivery time can be an important competitive factor alongside price and ratings.

---

## 5. Key Findings

### 💰 Pricing

Central Biryani has the lowest listed price in the selected sample at ₹130.

Nagerkovil Arya Bhavan has the highest listed price at ₹350.

### ⭐ Customer Ratings

Yadha Foods has the highest rating in the selected sample at 4.7/5.

Night Lion has the lowest rating in the selected sample at 3.9/5.

### 🚴 Delivery

HMR Biriyani Restaurant has the shortest estimated delivery time in the sample at 9 minutes.

Yadha Foods has the longest estimated delivery time at 55 minutes.

---

## 6. Business Insights

Based on the sample:

1. Restaurants compete across a broad price range.
2. Competitive pricing can attract price-sensitive customers.
3. Strong ratings can support customer trust even at mid-range prices.
4. Faster delivery can provide an additional competitive advantage.
5. Restaurants can benchmark their pricing and service levels against nearby competitors.

---

## 7. Recommendations

For a restaurant entering or competing in this market:

- Maintain competitive pricing within the target segment.
- Monitor competitor menu prices regularly.
- Focus on maintaining strong customer ratings.
- Improve delivery speed where possible.
- Track competitor offers and promotions.
- Use customer ratings and pricing together when evaluating competitors.

---

## 8. Limitations

This analysis is based on a limited sample of publicly available restaurant listings.

Prices, ratings, delivery estimates, availability, and offers may change over time.

The results should therefore be treated as a snapshot of the market rather than a permanent representation of pricing.
